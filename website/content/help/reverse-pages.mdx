# Reverse Pages

Reverse the order of every page in one PDF. Page content and rotation stay the same.

## Reverse and save

1. Choose **Reverse Pages** and open your PDF.
2. Check the **New page order** message.
3. Click **Save Reversed PDF…** and choose a separate output file.

A four-page input becomes pages 4, 3, 2, and 1. A single-page PDF keeps the same page order.

## Scope

The entire document is reversed; this tool has no selected-page mode. To work with only part of a document, [extract those pages](/docs/extract-pages) first. To turn a page upside down, use [Rotate PDF](/docs/rotate-pdf).

Your original stays unchanged. Check bookmarks, forms, and other advanced features in the saved copy.
