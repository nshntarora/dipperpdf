# Rotate PDF

Correct sideways or upside-down pages without changing the page order. Rotating changes the page's orientation metadata.

## Rotate and save

1. Choose **Rotate PDF** and open your PDF.
2. Select thumbnails for the pages to turn. Command-click adds or toggles pages; Shift-click selects a range.
3. Alternatively, enter a selection such as `1–3, 5` in **Page numbers**, then click **Apply** or press Return. Invalid entries leave the current selection unchanged.
4. Click **Rotate left 90°** or **Rotate right 90°**. Repeat for a half turn.
5. Check the thumbnails, then click **Rotate PDF** and inspect the output preview.
6. Click **Save PDF…** and save a separate copy.

Use **Select All** to rotate the whole document or **Clear** to clear the selection. Preparation is available once a page has a changed rotation; a full 360-degree turn returns it to the original orientation. Changing the selection alone keeps applied rotations and the prepared result; applying another rotation clears the result.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **Command+A** selects all pages when you are not editing **Page numbers**. While editing that field, it selects the field's text.
- **Command+Shift+L** rotates selected pages left.
- **Command+Shift+R** rotates selected pages right.
- **Command+Return** prepares the rotated PDF.
- **Command+S** saves the prepared PDF.

The source file stays unchanged. To change last-to-first order instead of orientation, use [Reverse Pages](/docs/reverse-pages).
