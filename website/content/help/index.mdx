# Documentation

DipperPDF handles everyday PDF tasks on your Mac. Your documents stay local, and each tool saves a separate result so your originals stay unchanged.

Start with [Install](/docs/install), then follow [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) to make your first PDF copy. Choose a tool guide below for its controls, output, and limits.

Need an answer quickly? Read the [FAQs](/docs/faq) or [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
