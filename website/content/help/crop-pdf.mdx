# Crop PDF

Adjust the visible area of a page by trimming its margins. You can crop all pages or a consecutive range.

<DocsTableOfContents entries={toc} />

## Crop and preview

1. Choose **Crop PDF** and open your PDF.
2. Enter **Top margin (pt)**, **Bottom margin (pt)**, **Left margin (pt)**, and **Right margin (pt)**. There are 72 points in an inch.
3. Set **Page range** using **From** and **To**. The initial range includes every page.
4. Click **Crop PDF** and inspect the output preview using its page arrows. The settings diagram is schematic.
5. Click **Save PDF…** to save a separate copy.

Margins trim the current visible edges, including on rotated pages. Pages outside your range stay unchanged. For example, 36 points trims half an inch from an edge.

## Limits

Use nonnegative margins that leave a visible area on every affected page. If **Crop PDF** is disabled, check for invalid numeric entries or fix the page range. If preparation reports that the margins leave no visible area, reduce them and try again.

> Cropping hides content without deleting it. Hidden text or images may still be recoverable. Do not use cropping to redact sensitive information.

Changing settings clears the prepared result; prepare it again before saving. [Remove Pages](/docs/remove-pages) removes whole pages instead of trimming their edges. Use [Redact PDF](/docs/redact-pdf) to find and remove supported selectable text.
