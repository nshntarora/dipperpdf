# Crop PDF

Adjust the visible area of a page by trimming its margins. You can crop all pages or a consecutive range.

## Crop and preview

1. Choose **Crop PDF** and open your PDF.
2. Enter **Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, and **Right** margins in points. There are 72 points in an inch.
3. Set **First page** and **Last page**. The initial range includes every page.
4. Click **Preview Cropped PDF** and inspect the page previews.
5. Click **Save PDF…** to save a separate copy.

Margins trim the current visible edges, including on rotated pages. Pages outside your range stay unchanged. For example, 36 points trims half an inch from an edge.

## Limits

Use nonnegative margins that leave a visible area on every affected page. If Preview is disabled, reduce the margins or fix the page range.

> Cropping hides content without deleting it. Hidden text or images may still be recoverable. Do not use cropping to redact sensitive information.

Changing settings clears the prepared result; preview again before saving. [Remove Pages](/docs/remove-pages) removes whole pages instead of trimming their edges.
