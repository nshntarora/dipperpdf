# Extract Pages

Save only the pages you choose as one new PDF. The original document keeps all of its pages.

## Select and export

1. Choose **Extract Pages** and open your PDF.
2. Click a thumbnail to select a page. Command-click adds or toggles pages; Shift-click selects a range.
3. Check the selected-page count. Use **Select All** or **Clear Selection** if needed.
4. Click **Save Selected Pages…** and choose a separate filename and location.

Select at least one page to enable saving. Selecting pages 2, 5, and 6 produces a three-page PDF in that order, regardless of the order you clicked them.

## Choose the right tool

Extract Pages produces one PDF. Use [Split PDF](/docs/split-pdf) to create multiple consecutive files, or [Remove Pages](/docs/remove-pages) to save everything except your selection.

Review the saved document before sharing it, especially if it contains forms, bookmarks, or signatures.
