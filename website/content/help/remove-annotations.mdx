# Remove Annotations

Remove comments, highlights, text boxes, drawings, and stamps from every page. Save a separate copy to keep the reviewed original.

## Clear review markup

1. Choose **Remove Annotations** and open your PDF.
2. Read the summary of what will be removed and kept.
3. Click **Save Without Annotations…** and save a new PDF.
4. Open the result to confirm the markup you wanted removed is gone.

## What remains

Links and form fields are kept. The tool works on PDF annotations; marks already flattened into page content cannot be removed. It applies to every page and has no individual-annotation selector.

This is not a redaction tool or a complete document sanitizer. Review the output for remaining visible or embedded information before sharing. [Edit PDF Metadata](/docs/edit-pdf-metadata) can separately change the title, author, subject, and keywords.
