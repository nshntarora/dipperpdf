# Remove Pages

Choose pages to leave out, then save a PDF containing the remaining pages. Your original remains intact.

<DocsTableOfContents entries={toc} />

## Remove selected pages

1. Choose **Remove Pages** and open your PDF.
2. Click thumbnails for the pages to remove. Command-click adds or toggles pages; Shift-click selects a range.
3. Alternatively, enter a selection such as `2, 4` in **Page numbers**, then click **Apply** or press Return. Invalid entries leave the current selection unchanged.
4. Check the **to remove** and **remaining** counts.
5. Click **Remove Pages** and review the remaining pages in the output preview.
6. Click **Save PDF…** and save a separate copy.

Removing pages 2 and 4 from a five-page document leaves original pages 1, 3, and 5 in that order.

## If removal is disabled

Select at least one page to remove and keep at least one page in the document. If you selected every page, deselect one or use **Clear** and start again. Changing the selection clears the prepared result; prepare it again to make **Save PDF…** available.

To save just a few chosen pages instead, use [Extract Pages](/docs/extract-pages).
