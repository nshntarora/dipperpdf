# Remove Pages

Choose pages to leave out, then save a PDF containing the remaining pages. Your original remains intact.

## Remove selected pages

1. Choose **Remove Pages** and open your PDF.
2. Click thumbnails for the pages to remove. Command-click adds or toggles pages; Shift-click selects a range.
3. Check the **to remove** and **remaining** counts.
4. Click **Save PDF Without Selected Pages…** and save a separate copy.

Removing pages 2 and 4 from a five-page document leaves original pages 1, 3, and 5 in that order.

## If saving is disabled

Select at least one page to remove and keep at least one page in the document. If you selected every page, deselect one or use **Clear Selection** and start again.

To save just a few chosen pages instead, use [Extract Pages](/docs/extract-pages).
