# Add Page Numbers

Stamp consecutive numbers onto a page range. The number at the first page in the range is the starting number you choose.

## Number and save

1. Choose **Add Page Numbers** and open your PDF.
2. Choose **Position**, then set **First page** and **Last page**.
3. Enter a positive **Starting number** and choose a **Font size** from 8 to 32 points.
4. Click **Preview Numbered PDF** and inspect the placement.
5. Click **Save PDF…** and save a new copy.

For example, setting the range to pages 3–5 and starting number to 1 stamps those pages with 1, 2, and 3. Pages outside the range receive no added numbers.

## Placement and limits

Black numbers sit 24 points from the visible bottom edge, at the selected position. They can overlap existing content; check the previews before saving. This adds numbers to the page content rather than changing the document's logical page labels or replacing existing printed numbers.

Use a range within the PDF and a positive starting number to enable Preview. Changing settings clears the result, so preview again before saving.

For a combined report, [merge the PDFs](/docs/merge-pdfs) first, then number the saved result.
